<h3><strong>Youth for Environmental Sustainability Festival (YES Fest) 2026</strong></h3>
<p><strong>10–12 July 2026 | Kallang Wave Mall (Atrium and Lawn) | Admission: Free</strong>
</p>
<p>Singapore's youth are not waiting for tomorrow. They are already building
it!</p>
<p>YES Fest 2026 brings together young environmental changemakers from Institutes
of Higher Learning, who have completed NEA’s YES Leaders Programme. The
YES Leaders will be showcasing their sustainability projects at Kallang
Wave Mall for three days only! Do drop by YES Fest, have lots of fun, and
walk away with ideas and skills to upcycle and reuse everyday items. Admission
is free.</p>
<p>See you there!</p>
<h4><strong>What's In Store for You</strong></h4>
<p>🔍 Explore real-world sustainability projects driven by YES Leaders who
are already making a difference in their schools and communities and get
inspired to start your own journey.</p>
<p>🛠️ Get hands-on at our free workshops organised by our passionate YES
Leaders and walk away with practical skills you can use from the very next
day. Slots are limited, so sign up early!</p>
<p>🎁 Redeem exclusive merchandise on the spot, a little something to remind
you that your actions matter.</p>
<p>🎟️ Try your luck at our Gachapon prize machine for instant prizes!</p>
<p>📸 Meet Captain Green on 11 and 12 July from 4.00pm to 6.00pm!</p>
<p>YES Fest welcomes everyone including adults, families, and youth. It is
one of the key events of the Youth for Environmental Sustainability (YES)
Movement, organised by the National Environment Agency of Singapore (NEA).</p>
<h4><strong>YES Explorer Zone: Youth Doing Their Part for Sustainability</strong></h4>
<p>Meet the young Singaporeans who saw an opportunity to make a difference
and decided to do something about it.</p>
<p>Our YES Explorer Zone puts the spotlight on YES Leaders who took on real
environmental challenges in their communities and came out with something
remarkable to show for it. Their projects are bold, creative, and deeply
personal, and every single one started with a simple decision to try.</p>
<p>Walk through the exhibition, hear their stories, and let their journeys
spark something in you. You might just leave with an idea of your own.</p>
<p>While you are here, join our YES Sustainability Quest! Hunt for hidden
clues tucked within the exhibits, answer questions based on what you discover,
and rack up points for a chance to win exciting prizes. Fun for the whole
family and a great way to dig deeper into the stories on display.</p>
<h4><strong>Workshops: Small Skills, Big Impact</strong></h4>
<p>Sustainability starts with small, everyday actions, and our free workshops
show you how. No experience needed, no jargon, no pressure. Just practical,
enjoyable sessions that give you real skills to bring home and use straight
away.</p>
<p>Come solo, bring a friend, or make it a family outing. Every skill you
pick up here is a step towards a greener Singapore.</p>
<p>Spaces fill up fast, so don't wait. Secure your spot today!</p>

<p><strong>Before You Come: Good to Know</strong>
</p>
<p>1.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Registration is on a first-come, first-served
basis. Your spot is only confirmed upon receipt of a confirmation email
from NEA.</p>
<p>2.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Each registration is for a maximum of
two participants (adult or child combinations): 1 adult and 1 child, or
2 adults.</p>
<p>3.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; NEA reserves the right to amend, postpone,
or cancel any workshop without prior notice.</p>
<p>4.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Registration for all workshops closes
on 5 July 2026 at 11.59pm.</p>
<p>5.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Please arrive at least 10 minutes before
your session begins and present your confirmation email on-site. NEA reserves
the right to release reserved places if participants do not arrive 5 minutes
before the session starts.</p>
<p><strong>Parental Consent</strong>
</p>
<p>Children below the age of 12 must be accompanied by at least one parent
or guardian throughout the workshop.</p>
