<p>Behind every survivor lies a story that reminds us: this is no small matter.
Hear from our survivors below and take action to protect yourself and your
loved ones. Mosquito-borne diseases can lead to severe health consequences,
but our collective efforts to keep mosquito breeding low can prevent these
stories from becoming yours
<br>
<br>If you are a dengue survivor, we encourage you to share your story on
social media with the hashtag #BLOCKDengue to alert others of the dangers
of dengue.&nbsp;</p>
