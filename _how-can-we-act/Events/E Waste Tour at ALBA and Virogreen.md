<h3>E-Waste Tour at ALBA and Virogreen                </h3>
<p>📆 5 Jun to 11 Dec 2026, 1.30PM to 5.00PM
<br>📍 ALBA E-Waste Smart Recycling Pte Ltd/Virogreen Singapore Pte Ltd</p>
<p>Discover Singapore's comprehensive approach to electronic waste management
through this educational tour that combines learning with real-world application.
Begin at the ALBA Visitor Centre, where interactive exhibits teach you
about e-waste's environmental impact and proper recycling techniques, then
continue to Virogreen to witness the actual processing of electronic waste—from
dismantling devices to recovering precious metals and safely handling hazardous
materials.</p>
<p>Online registration opens on 11 May 2026 at 9AM.</p>
