<h2>Sea Exploration Tour</h2>
<p>📆 20 Jun 2026 to 13 Nov 2026, 9.30am to 1pm
<br>📍Marina South Pier</p>
<p>Set sail on an exclusive 3.5-hour Sea Exploration Tour into the heart
of the Singapore Strait, one of the world's busiest waterways!</p>
<p>Begin with a guided tour of the Singapore Maritime Gallery before setting

Islands Marine Park. Explore lush trails, tidal pools, and discover innovative
conservation efforts including solar-powered facilities and a floating
boardwalk.</p>
<p>Join us for an unforgettable voyage celebrating Singapore's maritime legacy,
