<h3>ECO PADDLE WITH OLA</h3>
<p>📆 24 May to 15 Nov, 10AM to 12PM
<br>📍 Ola Beach Club, Sentosa</p>
<p>Learn about Singapore’s marine biodiversity, the impact of ocean pollution,
and practical ways to protect marine ecosystems.</p>
<p>Embark on a guided kayak journey along the shores of Sentosa and the nearby
beach, combining adventure with environmental action.</p>
<p>Participants will paddle along the coastline while conducting a hands-on
coastal clean-up, helping to remove marine debris from beaches and our
waters. A short land-based saftey and paddling briefing will be conducted
before the session starts.</p>
<p>This experience offers a meaningful opportunity to connect with nature
while contributing directly to the preservation of our coastal environment.</p>
<p>Online registration opens on 11 May 2026 at 9AM.</p>
