<p>Kindness Day SG 2026 is a national movement that celebrates and encourages
everyday acts of kindness, reminding us that even the smallest gestures
can contribute to building a more gracious society.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Held annually in May, the celebration in 2026 carries the theme: " Let's
Be Greater Together ". This year, Kindness Day SG takes the festivities
to the heartlands with three neighbourhood activations held simultaneously
across Singapore, bringing the spirit of kindness and environmental care
closer to our communities.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Come with your families and immerse yourselves in fun and meaningful activities
that inspire kindness towards our loved ones, our community, and the environment
we all share. Pedal your way through our energy-generating cycling challenge,
and test your recycling knowledge with our interactive game.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>Join us for a day of excitement, discovery, and collective action as we
each play our part in making Singapore a greater place for all.</p>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
<p>For more details, please visit <a href="https://www.kindness.sg/get-involved#kindness-day-sg" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" target="_blank">Singapore Kindness Movement - Be Greater</a>
</p>
