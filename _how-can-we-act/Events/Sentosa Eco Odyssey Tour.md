<h3>Sentosa Eco-Odyssey Tour</h3>
<p>📆 30 May to 28 Nov 2026, 9.30AM to 12.30PM
<br>📍 Sentosa</p>
<p>The Sentosa Eco-Odyssey Tour offers participants an immersive tour of
four highlights from the island’s innovative sustainability journey:
<br>
<br>• Sentosa Nature Discovery - guests embark on an interactive exploration
that uncovers the rich biodiversity and ongoing conservation efforts shaping
Sentosa’s natural landscape.
<br>• Siloso Beach Resort - where participants experience firsthand the resort's
sustainable hospitality practices and impactful environmental initiatives.
<br>• Sentosa Cool Node - visitors witness climate adaptation in action, with
creative solutions designed to tackle heat stress and foster a more comfortable
public space.
<br>• Microforest - this segment offers a close-up look at how small-scale
reforestation projects contribute to Sentosa’s green vision, providing
both educational insights and inspiration for sustainable living.
<br>
<br>Online registration opens on 11 May 2026 at 9AM.
