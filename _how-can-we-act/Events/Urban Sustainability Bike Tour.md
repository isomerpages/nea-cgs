<h3>Urban Sustainability Bike Tour</h3>
<p>📆 30 May to 28 Nov 2026, 5PM to 7.30PM
<br>📍 Telok Ayer MRT Station Exit A (Start Point)</p>
<p>Pedal through Singapore's remarkable environmental success story on this
guided cycling tour along the Singapore River. Witness firsthand how a
once-polluted waterway was transformed into today's vibrant, clean corridor
through innovative government initiatives like the ABC Waters Programme
and collective community action.
<br>
<br>As you cycle past iconic landmarks, you'll discover Singapore's climate
adaptation strategies, track your own carbon footprint, and learn how individual
actions contribute to our nation's green future.
<br>
<br>Online registration opens on 11 May 2026 at 9AM.
