<h2>Maritime Discovery Expedition</h2>
<p>📆 30 May 2026 to 13 Nov 2026, 10am to 12pm
<br>📍 Singapore Maritime Gallery</p>
<p>Embark on an exclusive Maritime Discovery Expedition for an up-close look
at Singapore's cutting-edge maritime industry!</p>
<p>Begin with a guided tour of the Singapore Maritime Gallery, where you
will discover Singapore's transformation into a global maritime hub, the
vital role of shipping in our daily lives, and groundbreaking decarbonisation
efforts shaping a more sustainable future. Then, step into the future aboard
Pyxis One — Singapore's first fully electric harbour craft. Explore how
this innovative vessel operates, get up close to its charging facilities,
and enjoy a smooth, quiet joy ride into Singapore Harbour.</p>
<p>Join us for an unforgettable journey celebrating maritime innovation,
sustainability, and excellence!</p>
