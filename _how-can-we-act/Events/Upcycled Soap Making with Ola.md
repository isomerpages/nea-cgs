<h3>Upcycled Soap Making with Ola</h3>
<p>📆 23 May to 24 Oct 2026, 11.30AM TO 1PM
<br>📍 Ola Beach Club, Sentosa</p>
<p>Learn how everyday soap scraps get a second life. A hands-on class that's
creative, sustainable and fun for all ages.
<br>
<br>Discover how everyday soap scraps can be transformed into beautiful, usable
bars with a second life.
<br>
<br>This hands-on workshop introduces participants to the art of upcycled
soap making, blending creativity with sustainability in a fun, accessible
format for all ages.
<br>
<br>Participants will learn how to collect, clean, melt, and reshape leftover
soap pieces into new custom soaps. Along the way, they’ll explore color
blending, scent layering, and simple design techniques, creating unique
bars to take home.
