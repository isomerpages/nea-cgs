<h3>Microgreen Planting with Ola</h3>
<p>📆 3 May to 14 Nov 2026, 11.30AM TO 1.00PM
<br>📍 Ola Beach Club, Sentosa</p>
<p>Let's begin a planting adventure with our Microgreen Planting Activity!</p>
<p>We are learning how to help the Earth by using less waste and growing
tiny plants called microgreens. Discover how simple and exciting it is
to grow their own little garden at home!</p>
<p>Join us for a hands-on activity where you can plant your own microgreens,
bring them home to observe their growth, and later harvest them to add
to your own meals.</p>
<p>Participants will get to enjoy a small salad bowl to learn how microgreens
can be used in healthy recipes.</p>
