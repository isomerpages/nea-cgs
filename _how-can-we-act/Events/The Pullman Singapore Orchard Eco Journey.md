<h3>The Pullman Singapore Orchard Eco Journey</h3>
<p>📆 22 May to 14 Aug 2026, 2PM to 4PM
<br>📍 Pullman Singapore Orchard</p>
<p>At Pullman Singapore Orchard, sustainability comes to life through an
immersive learning journey in partnership with Corridor Farmers and the
National Environment Agency.
<br>
<br>This experience takes participants behind the scenes to discover how hotels
drive energy efficiency, reduce waste, and engage communities—from food
waste management to hydroponics and back-of-house practices.
<br>
<br>More than a tour, it’s a story of how small actions can create meaningful
impact—empowering guests to be part of a more sustainable future.
<br>
<br>Online registration opens on 11 May 2026 at 9AM.
